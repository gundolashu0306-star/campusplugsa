Campus Plug SA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

1. Introduction

Campus Plug SA respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect:

  • Name and surname.
  • Email address and account identification information.
  • Educational institution and campus information.
  • Student verification documents and registration details.
  • Profile photographs and other uploaded content.
  • Marketplace and accommodation listing information.
  • Messages, comments and community contributions.
  • Transaction and order information.
  • Technical information such as device and application data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Verify enrolled students where applicable.
  • Connect students with educational opportunities.
  • Facilitate accommodation and marketplace services.
  • Enable communication and student communities.
  • Process and manage orders and payments where available.
  • Improve application performance and security.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse and unauthorised access.

4. Account Registration and Authentication

Campus Plug SA supports account authentication, including Google Sign-In where available. Authentication services may be provided through Firebase Authentication. Information is processed to securely create and manage user accounts.

5. Data Storage and Security

Campus Plug SA uses Firebase services, including Cloud Firestore, for application data where configured. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared where necessary with:

  • Service providers supporting application operations.
  • Authentication and cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Payment providers where payment services are used.
  • Advertising providers where advertisements are displayed.
  • Relevant authorities where legally required.

7. Third-Party Services

Campus Plug SA may integrate third-party services, including:

  • Google Firebase services.
  • Google Sign-In.
  • Google AdMob advertising services, where enabled.
  • External websites and accommodation platforms.
  • Third-party payment providers where applicable.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers.

8. Advertising

Campus Plug SA may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may use device identifiers and other information in accordance with their privacy policies and applicable advertising consent requirements.

9. User-Generated Content

Users may publish posts, comments, marketplace listings, accommodation information and messages. Users are responsible for the information they choose to share through the application.

Campus Plug SA may moderate or remove content that violates its platform rules or applicable laws.

10. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, fulfil legitimate operational requirements and comply with applicable laws. When information is no longer required, appropriate deletion or anonymisation measures may be applied.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, users may:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise concerns about how their information is processed.

12. Children's Privacy

Campus Plug SA is designed primarily for students and individuals seeking educational opportunities. Users under the age of 18 should use the platform in accordance with applicable laws and any required parental or guardian consent.

13. South African Data Protection

Campus Plug SA aims to handle personal information in accordance with applicable South African privacy legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
